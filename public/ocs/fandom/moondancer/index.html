Moon-Dancer is a person who often struggles with her purpose. As terrible as it for everyone else, the Oblivion Crisis has given her one: help Martin Septim close shut the jaws of Oblivion and help him ascend to the throne of the Imperial Empire. But what happens to someone who is so dependent of their purpose when its suddenly robbed from them, along with the person they loved the most?

Personality

Moon-Dancer comes off as humble yet excitable, naive and sincere. She tends to switch rapidly between trusting people at their word and mistrusting everything someone tells her. Truthfully, she constantly fights extreme mood swings and volatile thoughts. She can be somewhat of a people-pleaser, as she is extremely concerned with making sure people like her and don’t want to hurt her. She’s somewhat selfish, as she considers her own feelings and safety first. She struggles to interact with people, as she overthinks how they might feel about her. She gets attached easily to people she feels understand her and this has resulted in a codependent attachment to Martin. She finds change difficult to adapt to and can easily collapse into depression after drastic changes in her life.

History

Moon-Dancer’s birth family was neglectful of her, which instilled into her the idea she can’t rely on anyone but herself. She ran away from home as a teenager and joined a temple a few villages over, intent not to worship but to learn their Claw-Dances to defend herself. After a few years, she joined a caravan network traveling Elsweyr and the nearby provinces.

In Cyrodiil, some years after, she accidentally killed a man who was harassing her caravan and was promptly sent to the Imperial Prison. While she had been there for a few weeks, she was moved to the cell containing the escape route only the night before the Emperor died. As in canon, she escaped when the Emperor was being led out of the city, heard his last words and received the Amulet of Kings. She ferried it immediately to Jauffre and then to find Martin, who she bonded with immediately on their shared complex relationship with the gods.

She happily helped Martin the entire way, growing a queerplatonic and codependent relationship with him that was mutual (though he recognized, to some extent, how dependent she was on him). In her downtime, she would either rove around helping people, doing Fighter’s Guild jobs or scaling the ranks of the Mage’s Guild.

Unfortunately, when Mehrunes Dagon invaded and Martin subsequently sacrificed himself to banish him, Moon-Dancer was shattered. In the immediate aftermath, Jauffre attempted to comfort her but she attacked him, only able to pin the blame on all this on him for losing the amulet in the first place. She came short of mauling him to death before she came to, realized what she had done and bolted, escaping into the wilderness thanks to the confusion. It was in this desperate, suicidal state she found the door in the Niben Bay.

In the Shivering Isles, she found herself going to Sheogorath who gave her a tempting proposal: if she hurt so much being Moon-Dancer, why doesn’t she just...become someone else? So she ended up helping him, not because she cared about the plight of the Shivering Isles but because she thought it would purge her of her pain. And as a result, her fate as the future Madgod was sealed.

Skills

Moon-Dancer is adept at Claw-Dances, the Khajiiti form of martial arts, having trained with it for some years. She also uses Destruction and Illusion magic proficiently, the former for defense and the latter to help her lackluster social skills.

She also loves to sew and make jewelry and often makes simple pieces in her downtime.

More References


Martin Septim

Moon hit it off with Martin right away and, feeling bereft of purpose, immediately threw herself into his cause. The pair grew close with both confiding in each other in such a time of crisis. In a way, Moon fell in love with Martin, planning on devoting herself to him as his confidant and bodyguard when he became Emperor. Unfortunately, when Martin sacrificed himself, it broke her.

Sheogorath

Moon was undoubtedly preyed upon by the Daedra in her most vulnerable time. In a sense, she understood this but followed him anyway. His promise that helping him stop the Greymarch by mantling him would make the pain go away was just too tantalizing.

Trivia

She likes to collect Welkynd Stones; not to use but because she thinks they’re pretty.

Moon-Dancer’s Amulet of Akatosh is a gift from Martin that was once his own. She ended up giving him a woven bracelet with beads and metal rings. It’s deeply sentimental to her but, as it is a depiction of Akatosh, becomes something she feels complicated about Post-Oblivion Crisis. Sometimes, after, she would talk to it as if it was Akatosh.

Moon-Dancer made her outfit with the vision of her one day being the body guard to Martin as the Emperor; thus she incorporated a purple/red/gold color scheme, red diamonds and some Cyrodiilic flourishes. It’s only by sheer coincidence it also shares a color scheme with Sheogorath...

Sometimes, when she’s feeling really panicked, she casts an Invisibility spell and can’t become visible again until she calms down.

Queerplatonic Relationship Questionairre by warimimama - Martin and Moon

1. What made them realize “Oh. You’re my person”?

I think right off the bat, when they met in Kvatch, they saw something in each other about their mutual complicated relationship with the gods! At least, Moon-Dancer felt like she could extend a bit of trust to Martin because of that. I bet for Martin it was seeing what measures Moon was willing to go for his protection (both what she went through to get to him in the first place and also defending him on the way to Cloud Ruler Temple). It’s pretty important to their dynamic that it developed VERY quickly.

2. How do they explain their bond to people who don’t quite ‘get it’, if they explain at all?

I feel for Martin, he sticks to “just very good friends” because he’s more concerned with warding off concerns that their relationship is romantic. He doesn’t want people thinking that and start telling him he’ll have to break it off once he becomes Emperor. As for Moon, she sticks to “my best friend” publically because she really isn’t interested in justifying the fact it’s more like “soulmate” to her.

3. What word or phrase do they use to describe each other—partner, soulmate, best friend, etc.?

Like I said, Martin sticks to “best friend” while Moon uses “soulmate”. Martin doesn’t have any issues with the mismatch, he probably teases Moon and calls her sentimental!

4. How do they celebrate their “anniversary”, if they have one?

I haven’t actually hammered out the Oblivion timeline but I don’t think they made it to a year...or, if they did, there was just too much going on to celebrate. It’s likely Moon brought back a cake or something to him and they just ate it together.

5. What’s a moment that solidified their commitment to each other?

So I said earlier, it was pretty obvious right off the bat how devoted Moon was to Martin. Unfortunately, she tends to be very isolated so their relationship turned codependent very quickly on her end. On Martin’s side, there was probably a moment one night at Cloud Ruler where he confided his anxieties to her in regards to becoming Emperor and she told him she’d remain at his side even then.

6. What’s something they trust each other with more than anyone else?

Their deepest feelings, largely. For Martin, it’s his anxieties of when he eventually becomes Emperor...does he even want to? Does he even have a choice? For Martin, it’s her psychosis. He might be one of a single-digit number of people who even knows about it.

7. What’s their dynamic as housemates or co-parents of plants/pets/kids?

Interesting question since there’s long stretches where they’re not with the other. I suppose when they are around, Moon tends to be attached at Martin’s hip because she was away for so long.

8. What’s something they’d never do without the other’s input?

I don't know if there's anything! Maybe if they actually got to have their relationship develop, Martin would have gotten Moon's input on certain decisions.

9. What’s something they’d never do without the other’s input?

Martin vows to be there in Moon’s life, despite what might come for either of them while Moon vows to remain with him and protect him as if his life was hers.

10. What’s something the other person does that always makes them feel safe?

For Martin, it’s easy because Moon has made it extremely clear she will defend him, even if it means she’s putting herself between him and the danger. For Moon, just listening to her without judgment.

11. Have they made future plans—travel, retirement, growing old together?

Yes! The plan was that when Martin became Emperor, Moon would become his personal knight/bodyguard as well as confidant. Of course, that didn’t come to fruition...

12. When did their relationship shift from platonic to something more?

There was a moment after Moon returned to Cloud Ruler Temple after encountering Glarthir. She sought comfort from him, which obviously came with talking about her psychosis. When he listened to her without judgment, that was when she realized whatever it was she felt, it wasn’t just platonic.

13. Who initiates comfort more—and what does that look like?

Definitely Moon. It started with just talking then grew into more physical touch until it became her laying with him to help her sleep, since she’s prone to insomnia. That last part is a bit of an open secret around Cloud Ruler Temple.

14. How do they prioritize each other in their lives?

Martin becomes the person Moon always comes to to talk and vice versa! Moon is also always on the lookout for things to bring back to him so he’s kind of constantly on her mind while she’s traveling.

15. What’s something that annoys them about each other but in a “you’re stuck with me” sort of way?

I think Moon tends to stare right at people when they talk and at first it really bothered Martin until it grew on him. On the flip side, I feel Martin would be really pedantic and it would drive Moon up the wall!

16. What’s a mundane task they like doing together?

Cooking! I think Moon would miss a lot of Khajiiti dishes and make an effort to cook them when she’s at the temple and of course share them with Martin. I could also see them reading together, whether Moon is actively reading with him or he’s reading out loud while she’s sewing.

17. What makes their relationship feel like home?

No matter how long Moon is away, when she returns Martin is there willing—and happy!—to be there for her.

18. How do they show affection in public versus private?

Publically, or at least in most of the Temple, it’s just spending time together, maybe hand-touching or hugging. Privately, Moon likes to cuddle with Martin, both for comfort and just because they want to.

19. What kind of boundaries have they set, and how did they figure them out?

So I don’t think many boundaries were actually formally set. On Moon’s side, it never occurs to her to set any once she trusts Martin completely. In fact, she probably sees no reason to set any. If Martin wasn’t such a good guy, she could very easily be taken advantage of. On Martin’s side, they probably had to work out to more physical shows of affection—both so he could figure out if he was even okay with them and if he cared what the Blades thought about it.

20. Do they live together, want to, or have any shared domestic routines?

Unfortunately, during the entirety of their relationship, Martin is effectively on house arrest in Cloud Ruler Temple and Moon is spending long stretches of time traveling, fetching him the items they need to open the portal to Paradise. In Moon’s fantasies, she thinks she’d like to live in the White-Gold Tower with him. Separate living spaces, so to not encourage rumors, but allowed to pass through each other’s spaces freely.

21. How do they talk about each other when the other isn’t around?

Very positively! Martin always talks about how dependable and brave Moon is while Moon gushes on and on about how amazing and great and wonderful her good friend is. Though, Martin also worries privately about Moon. He kinda sees how attached she is to him and...isn’t necessarily upset by it but thinks she needs to have more friends than just him. Y’know...just in case things don’t go exactly the way she wants them to.

22. How do they define “intimacy” and how does that show up in their bond?

Both trust the other with their deepest secrets and stuff they just can’t tell other people. For Moon there’s the extra aspect of how she craves physical affection from him because she knows he wouldn’t try to hurt her.

23. What’s a small gesture that never fails to make the other person light up?

Moon knows how stuffy it is to be stuck in the temple so she always lugs back all sorts of things back for Martin; interesting treats that’ll survive the trek, new books, knickknacks and trinkets. Martin feels really seen by her doing that. For Moon, it’s Martin making or bringing her food when she returns. They have a whole ritual about it when she comes back to the temple.

24. What random thing do they always “argue” about, even if they know it doesn’t matter?

I think part of the aforementioned ritual is Martin griping at her about where he’s going to put all the stuff she brings him. She insists she will find a place but all the stuff eventually ends up spilling into her room and then the main hall.

25. What do they insist doing “as a team”, even if its wildly inefficient?

Honestly, I can’t think of anything! I think pretty early on they establish that there’s things Martin can do and things Moon can do but these things don’t really overlap. And everything else is mundane enough that they’re a good team!

26. What’s the dumbest thing they’ve done out of loyalty for the other?

Moon’s a lot more prone to this because how insanely dependent on Martin she is. During the Battle of Bruma, she definitely got really injured protecting Martin (then had to do a mad dash through the gate…). She got really chastised by Martin after the fact and was forced to go into Bruma to get a professional healer to look at it.

27. What’s something they enable in each other that no one else would tolerate?

I don’t think Martin would like anyone else to be attached at the hip like Moon is to him. Moon, having Martin on such a high pedestal, gives him a lot of passes for things that would upset her from anyone else.

28. What’s something they’ve “taught” each other, intentionally or not?

Moon was on the road to learning to trust people and letting them in. She was also probably becoming a little more trusting of the gods through Martin who in turn was becoming the same way, which also probably paved the way for him to make his sacrifice.

29. What’s their version of “I love you”, even if it’s never spoken?

"I'll miss you." or "I'll be waiting for you."

30. What’s a silly or playful part of their bond that no one else gets?

I think there's a lot of play arguments, like the aforementioned knick-knack argument! Others arguments include Moon's sweet tooth and how shes going to rot both their teeth with the sweets she brings and how her fur gets EVERYWHERE.

31. What’s a time one of them completely understood what the other needed without being told?

I think when Moon returned after her encounter with Glarthir, Martin understood immediately something was wrong and she needed to talked to and comforted.

32. What side of themselves do they only show in this relationship?

Vulnerability, mostly. Moon doesn’t trust anyone because her psychosis, believing anything she tells anyone will be used against her. (Doesn’t help when you and your qpp is being targeted by a very real doomsday cult…). Martin really doesn’t have any else to talk to about certain things beside her, since there’s an inherent power imbalance between him and the Blades.

33. What’s a disagreement they had that only made their bond stronger?

I think after the Battle of Bruma, Martin asked Moon to not throw away her life in his defense, that her putting herself in so much danger for his benefit upset him. She agreed….which makes the fact that, upon losing him, she becomes EXTREMELY suicidal all the more tragic.

34. How would others describe their relationship, even if they don’t understand it?

Once it became clear they weren’t romantic, I think the Blades would half-jokingly describe Moon as Martin’s “most loyal knight” which Moon quite liked the sound of. In the years after, this description persisted even after both were gone.

35. What’s a memory that both of them treasure, but rarely talk about?

I feel both have a lot of fond feelings towards their first meeting but since all the other stuff with Kvatch is bundled into that....its hard to talk about.
